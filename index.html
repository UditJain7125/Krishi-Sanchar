About Disease

Early blight is a common fungal disease that affects tomato leaves causing dark, concentric rings (target spots).

Treatment

  • Remove infected leaves immediately.
  • Spray neem oil or chlorothalonil fungicide.
  • Maintain proper drip irrigation to avoid wet leaves.

Prevention

  • Use disease resistant seeds.
  • Avoid overhead watering.
  • Rotate crops annually.
